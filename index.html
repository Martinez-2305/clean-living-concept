Standard clean

Perfect for keeping your home fresh and tidy.

  • Dusting all accessible surfaces

  • Sweeping and mopping hard floors
  • Kitchen worktops and sink
  • Wiping cupboard fronts
  • Outside of appliances

  • Emptying bins
  • General tidying
  • Light polishing of surfaces
  • Bed changing

Deep clean

A thorough top-to-bottom clean. Everything in the standard clean, plus:

  • Skirting boards
  • Doors, frames and handles
  • Light switches and sockets
  • Internal windows and sills
  • Detailed bathroom descaling
  • Kitchen splashbacks degreased
  • Behind and under movable furniture
  • Detailed dusting of all surfaces
  • Cobweb removal
  • Radiators dusted
  • Extra attention to high-touch areas
